# Establish Taipei New Year countdown evidence and refresh timing

## Question

What authoritative evidence is currently available for the 2026–2027 Taipei New Year countdown, viewing logistics, transit, closures, crowd controls, and weather exposure, and which facts must remain explicitly unconfirmed until later refresh checkpoints?

## Resolution comments

### 2026-07-28 — Resolve with a provisional event anchor and mandatory refresh gates

**Decision:** Phase 1 may treat the countdown itself as an announced must-do anchor, but it must not yet choose a viewing position, arrival route, exit station, or post-event timetable as fact. Every operational detail must carry its source year and verification state; a prior-year rule is evidence for uncertainty and refresh timing, never a 2026–2027 instruction.

#### Announced for 2026–2027 as of 2026-07-28

- The [official Taipei tourism event page](https://travel.taipei/en/event-calendar/details/66774), maintained by the Taipei City Government Department of Information and Tourism and updated 2026-07-28, lists the **Taipei New Year's Party 2027** for **2026-12-31 through 2027-01-01** at **Taipei City Hall and its surrounding square**. The corresponding [Traditional Chinese listing](https://travel.taipei/zh-tw/event-calendar/details/66403) says the party is planned for **19:00–01:00** and culminates in the Taipei 101 New Year fireworks/show. The [2027 official event calendar](https://www.travel.taipei/zh-tw/event-calendar/2027) warns that organizer updates control.
- Taipei 101's official procurement list contains tender **K11506003, “Taipei 101 New Year (2026–2027) fireworks display activity engineering,”** published 2026-06-08. This corroborates preparation, but neither the tender list nor Taipei 101's current [generic fireworks page](https://www.taipei-101.com.tw/tw/explore/fireshow) publishes the 2026–2027 theme, duration, technical format, public viewing plan, or cancellation policy.
- No authoritative source found currently announces specific performers or running order, fireworks duration/theme, ticket or admission rules, designated viewing zones, stage/entrance/accessibility map, security screening, road and pedestrian closures, YouBike/bus changes, special Metro hours/frequencies, station entrance/exit controls, dispersal routes, or event-day weather. These remain **unconfirmed**, not inherited from 2025–2026.

#### What prior official events establish—and do not establish

|---|---|---|

The completed event drew [about 200,000 people and had a six-minute fireworks show](https://www.travel.taipei/en/news/details/66616). | Candidate viewpoints must be compared on sightline, exposure, effort, toilets, crowding, and exit feasibility; large crowds are a demonstrated risk. | Availability, suitability, crowd level, view quality, fireworks duration, or attendance for 2027. |

#### Mandatory refresh checkpoints

| Gate | Required authoritative refresh | Planning consequence if still absent |
|---|---|---|
| **30 days before — 2026-12-01** | Recheck Taipei City tourism/Information and Tourism, the dedicated event site if launched, and Taipei 101 for the program, exact fireworks statement, admission/ticket rules, designated viewing options, accessible areas, and cancellations. The previous year's October/November performer releases make this a useful program checkpoint, not a guarantee. | Keep all missing facts visibly **unconfirmed**; do not lock a viewing location or non-refundable countdown-adjacent activity. |
| **7 days before — 2026-12-24** | Recheck the city event FAQ, Department of Transportation, Public Transportation Office, Taipei Metro, police/security notices, Taipei 101, and official viewing-site managers for entrance/stage maps, screening, toilets, accessibility, road/pedestrian/YouBike/bus controls, special rail service, and exit routes. Query the [Central Weather Administration's official seven-day forecast](https://opendata.cwa.gov.tw/promotion/introduction/forecast) for 12-hour rain probability, apparent temperature, wind, and weather. | A countdown plan cannot be marked reliable without an evidence-backed entry and exit path. Preserve at least one lower-exposure or indoor fallback and show unresolved risks. |
| **24 hours before — 2026-12-30/31** | Recheck organizer and operator updates, live Taipei Metro/traffic notices, closures and station exits, event cancellation/safety messages, then CWA's 72-hour/three-hour forecast, warnings, radar, and one-hour rain nowcast. Revalidate the exact walking path in case barriers change it. | Trigger same-day repair when weather, access, transit, or crowd controls differ; otherwise keep the countdown but require owner acknowledgement of the unresolved risk. |

The CWA's [winter climate guidance](https://climate.cwa.gov.tw/SeasonalClimate?subpage=Winter) says Taipei has historically averaged 2.4 December days affected by cold waves; this is climate context, **not** a forecast for 31 December 2026. The countdown is an outdoor late-night event, so rain, wind, visibility, and cold exposure must remain unknown until CWA forecast products enter range.

At each gate, store publisher, source URL, publication/update timestamp, retrieval timestamp, applicable event year, and status (`confirmed`, `historical pattern`, `conflicting`, or `unconfirmed`). The newest event-year notice from the responsible organizer or operator overrides aggregators and cached route results.
